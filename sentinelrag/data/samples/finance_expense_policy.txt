Finance Department - Expense Reimbursement Policy

All employee travel expenses exceeding 5,000 rupees require written
pre-approval from the requesting employee's department head before the
trip is booked. Expenses below this threshold may be submitted for
reimbursement after the fact, with itemized receipts, within 30 days.

International travel expenses are reimbursed at actual cost up to a cap
of 200,000 rupees per trip, inclusive of airfare, lodging, and per-diem
meal allowance of 3,000 rupees per day.

Client entertainment expenses require both the department head's
approval and a brief written justification describing the business
purpose, attached to the reimbursement request.

Any expense claim submitted more than 90 days after the expense date will
be rejected unless approved directly by the CFO.
