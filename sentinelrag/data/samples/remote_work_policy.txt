Remote Work Policy - Company Handbook, 2026 Edition

All full-time employees across every department are eligible to work
remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval submitted
through the HR portal at least 48 hours in advance.

Employees must remain reachable during core collaboration hours, defined
as 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, and attend all scheduled meetings with
video enabled unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

A one-time home office equipment stipend of 15,000 rupees is provided to
each eligible employee, covering desks, chairs, and monitors. Stipend
requests are processed by Finance within 15 business days of approval.

Employees working remotely from a different country for more than 14
consecutive days must notify HR and Legal in advance for tax and
compliance reasons.
