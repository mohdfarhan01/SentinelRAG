Executive Compensation Report - Confidential Board Briefing

Total compensation for the Chief Executive Officer for the current fiscal
year, including base salary, performance bonus, and equity grants, is
6.8 crore rupees.

The executive leadership team's combined compensation pool for the fiscal
year is 22.4 crore rupees, distributed across seven executive roles based
on individual performance reviews conducted by the Board Compensation
Committee.

A proposed 12% increase to executive base salaries for the next fiscal
year is under review pending Board approval at the upcoming quarterly
meeting, and has not yet been finalized or communicated externally.
